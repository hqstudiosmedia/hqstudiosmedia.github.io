Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

HQ Studios operates the channels Million Dollar Math and Physics Chaos and an internal publishing tool ("the App") that uploads our own videos to our own accounts on YouTube, Instagram/Facebook and TikTok.

What data the App uses

The App only accesses accounts owned by HQ Studios. Through the official APIs it uses: permission to upload and schedule videos, read basic account information (account name, ID) and read performance statistics (views, likes, comments, shares) of our own videos. It does not access data of any other user.

How data is stored

Access tokens and statistics are stored locally on our own computer and are never sold, shared or transferred to third parties. Statistics are used only to decide which videos we make next.

Visitors of this website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical data (such as IP addresses) as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Deletion

You can request deletion of any data relating to you by emailing robert.affiliate.page@gmail.com. Access granted to the App can be revoked at any time in the Google, Meta or TikTok account settings.
